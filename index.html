Our Story

Built for the way Indian businesses really operate.

Dashmize India is a private limited company headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, working with clients across India (PAN India). We were founded to close the gap between what off-the-shelf software offers and what growing Indian businesses actually need.

From our head office in Bengaluru and regional office in Darbhanga, we work with SMEs, manufacturers, hospitals, schools, and service businesses across the country to design, build, and support software that fits their exact workflows — not the other way around.

Every product we ship is built in-house by our own engineering team, backed by dedicated support long after launch.
